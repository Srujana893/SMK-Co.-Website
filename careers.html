Why work here

The work reaches you early.

In a single year you can move between a statutory audit, a GST assessment, a bank branch audit and an information systems review.

That breadth is the point. It is how professionals here learn to read a business rather than only its ledgers, and it is difficult to get in a larger firm where work is divided by department.

Every file is reviewed by a partner, and the review is a conversation: what the evidence supports, what is missing, and how the conclusion should be worded. That is the main way training happens here.

You can expect

  • Direct partner supervision
  • Client contact early
  • Exposure across practice areas
  • Structured file review
  • Support for qualifications that fit the work

We expect

  • Accuracy and documentation
  • Confidentiality without exception
  • Deadlines met
  • Willingness to keep learning
  • Standards and amendments kept current

How we work

Five stages, on every engagement.

We learn your business, obligations and goals before proposing anything.

Who keeps the records, how the numbers are produced, what the business is obliged to file and what has caused trouble before. Junior staff sit in on these conversations from the start.

A scoped engagement with clear deliverables, timelines and ownership.

Risk is assessed against real records and prior filings, then the work is scoped in writing. Everyone on the team knows what they own before fieldwork begins.

Partner-led work, supported by modern tools and tight documentation.

Fieldwork, sampling and testing to ICAI standards, with working papers prepared to stand up to later review by a bank, a buyer or the department.

Continued availability for filings, queries and the work that follows.

Notices, assessments and routine filings are handled as they arise, by the people who did the original work rather than a new team each time.

Each cycle informs the next.

At the close of a cycle the file is reviewed for what recurred — adjustments, weak controls, late records — and what should be tightened before the next year begins.
