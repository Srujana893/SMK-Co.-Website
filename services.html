The technology edge

Why a CA practice runs its own systems work.

The numbers come from systems now.

Most of our clients keep their books in software, so the figures are produced by systems. We examine those systems as part of the work.

  1. 01

    Systems are in scope.

    Who has access, how changes are made and how the figures are put together: audit, tax and compliance work looks at all three, not only at what comes out.

  2. 02

    Repetition is automated. Judgement is not.

    Where finance work is repetitive we automate it, so review and judgement get the time they need. Automated work is checked like any other, and a partner signs off the conclusion.

  3. 03

    One question, every engagement.

    From forensic work we bring the same question to every file: do the records show what actually happened?
